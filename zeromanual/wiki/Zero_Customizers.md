# Zero Customizers

This page catalogues various custom Zeros, whether one-offs or advertised as a service.

## [Hollywood Electrics](https://web.archive.org/web/2025/https://zeromanual.com/wiki/Hollywood_Electrics/Customs)

[The Hollywood Electrics Customs](https://hollywoodelectrics.com/collections/hollywood-electrics-customs) page showcases their main customization looks, with contact form for inquiries.

- [Cafe Primo](https://hollywoodelectrics.com/collections/hollywood-electrics-customs/products/cafe-primo) Zero FXS [Café Primo](https://www.instagram.com/p/BXBtZaKAz8H/?taken-by=hollywoodelectrics) cafe racer *(sold)*
  - Comes with a quarter fairing and windscreen, custom "tank", custom seat, and a leather storage pouch for one of the battery compartments.
  - [More Zero FXS Cafe Racer shots](https://www.instagram.com/p/BWqFdOXAkPs/?taken-by=hollywoodelectrics)
  - [Café Primo concept sketch](https://www.instagram.com/p/BWbMRz1g_y1/?taken-by=hollywoodelectrics)
- [Big Blue](https://hollywoodelectrics.com/collections/hollywood-electrics-customs/products/big-blue)
  - Harlan's personal custom 2013 Zero S(R). Dark blue paintjob with pinstriping and custom seat.
- [Zero Cafe](https://hollywoodelectrics.com/collections/hollywood-electrics-customs/products/zero-cafe)
  - Shortie tail, custom seat, custom round headlamp, bright white color.
- [Zero Carbon](https://hollywoodelectrics.com/collections/hollywood-electrics-customs/products/zero-carbon)
  - Showcases a set of available carbon-fiber weave replacements for the OEM plastics.
- [Zero Britten](https://hollywoodelectrics.com/collections/hollywood-electrics-customs/products/zero-britten)
  - Zero SR racing bike with custom fairings homage to the historic [Britten V1000](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Britten_V1000).
  - Bright colors, and integrated plastics sculpted across the top of the bike.
