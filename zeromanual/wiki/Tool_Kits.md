# Tool Kits

This is a reference for the tools needed to work on Zeros.

Some or many of these may be worth carrying with you on your bike, especially if you ride off-road or take trips far from home.

![Sample: Zero's former toolkit for 2013-2014](../images/Tool_kit_sample.jpg) *Sample: Zero's former toolkit for 2013-2014*

**Fastener Torques**

See [the owners' manuals](Official_Owners_Manual.md "Official Owners Manual"), and the [fastener specifications](Fastener_Specifications.md "Fastener Specifications") page.

## [Consumables](https://web.archive.org/web/2025/https://zeromanual.com/wiki/Consumables)

Here are some recommended spare parts to carry on your Zero or keep in your garage.

- Low voltage fuses (10 amp, 5 amp ATC style)
- High voltage fuses (ABC20A, ABC4A, ATM30 - see your [official owners' manual](Official_Owners_Manual.md "Official Owners Manual"))
- Spare [Drive Belt](https://web.archive.org/web/2025/https://zeromanual.com/wiki/Drive_Belt) (especially on pre-2017 models)

  Pre-ordering a belt can save replacement delivery delays when or if a belt snaps.
- Tire repair kit with the appropriate items to repair a tire (patch for tubes, plug for tubeless) and a pump or CO2 cartridge to refill the air.
