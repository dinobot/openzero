# Error Codes

Error codes are emitted by the [MBB](https://web.archive.org/web/2025/https://zeromanual.com/wiki/MBB) and displayed on the [Dash](https://web.archive.org/web/2025/https://zeromanual.com/wiki/Dash) or over the [OBD-II Port](https://web.archive.org/web/2025/https://zeromanual.com/wiki/OBD-II_Port).

The code interpretation varies from [Gen2](https://web.archive.org/web/2025/https://zeromanual.com/wiki/Gen2) to [Gen3](https://web.archive.org/web/2025/https://zeromanual.com/wiki/Gen3):
