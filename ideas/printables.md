# 3D printables for the project — survey 2026-11-09

Existing, published models relevant to OpenZero, with a proxy for "tested" (likes L / downloads D / makes M /
comments C, exact where the site exposes them). Grouped by component. Verdicts at the end: what to download,
what to remix, what has to be drawn from scratch because nothing exists.

Sites covered: Printables, Thingiverse, MakerWorld, Cults3D (search index only), Thangs, GrabCAD, GitHub,
zeromanual mirror, Endless Sphere, DIY Solar Forum, electricmotorcycleforum (Wayback). ~85 searches total.

## Gotion IFP52 cells (148 × 28.2 × 118.6 mm) — nothing exact exists

## Verdicts

**Download and print as-is (tested by others):**
1. Firskies TPU SB50 cap for the charge port until the Anderson boot arrives (or instead of it if the boot's 3 mm hinge
   version is remixed from the SB120 template).
2. AstroStock SB50 housing / tsukworks panel mount for the SB50 in the pack box wall or the stock charge-port location.
3. FRC strain relief or the MakerWorld handle on the pack-side SB50 (the 80 A lead is heavy, the plug gets yanked).
4. Adafruit Feather case with mounting tabs for the speed converter (verify USB-C cutout).

**Remix (source available, a one-evening job):**
5. WT29's f3d or Phill Bond's .scad → SB50 cap with lanyard sized to the port, tether to the panel mount.
6. BowenMarmot FreeCAD → terminal/busbar cover strip for the 2 × 15 Gotion rows (cell pitch 28.2 mm + gap,
   M6 nut capture, balance-lead slots). This is the one item worth the effort: 60 exposed M6 terminals.
7. CT-22 bracket: reuse the two-stud dimension from the E-Vader mount, draw a bar clamp for the FX's 22 mm bar.

**Draw from scratch (nothing exists, but simple):**
8. Ampseal 35 dust cap for the spare Sevcon connector positions — start from TE's STP.
9. ANT 30ZHB7 standoff plate (190 × 111 mm, holes to measure) with a slot for the Bluetooth module.
10. Class T fuse block cover (measure the block first — same MEASURE_ME as the enclosure).
11. FX module-bay filler / accessory tray on the stock module sliders 20-05773/20-05774 — nobody has done it.
12. Reed switch + magnet holder on the SB50 M4 holes for the charge-port sense path (idea from thing 7075647).

**Caveat on every SB50 model:** genuine Anderson housings differ from clones (thicker body, smaller rear slot, rib
positions). Print one, test on the actual plug, then adjust.
